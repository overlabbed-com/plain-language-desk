# Seeded document — clean band

This document exists to pin the scorer's green band. Every sentence stays at or
below 20 words. The voice stays active, and no sentence opens with there is.

## Why the fixture is clean

You can check each rule by hand. The words must, may, and should carry their
proper meanings, and the word shall never appears. No paragraph runs past one
hundred and fifty words, and the acronyms appear only in their whitelist form.

The flywheel reranks the worst files first. Then a rewrite clears the bar, and
the ledger records the rise.
